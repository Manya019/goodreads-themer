# Privacy Policy: Goodreads Glass Theme

**Last updated:** October 11, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Goodreads Glass Theme is a browser extension designed to customize the appearance of Goodreads, including its visual themes, colors, typography, and display settings.

Your privacy matters. This policy explains what information the extension accesses, how it is used, and how it is stored.

## 2. Information We Collect

Goodreads Glass Theme is designed to operate without requiring users to provide personal information, create an account, or submit identifying details to the extension developer.

The extension may access the following information as needed for its functionality:

- **Theme preferences:** Selected themes, colors, and other customization settings.
- **Extension settings:** Preferences required to maintain a consistent appearance across supported pages.
- **Page content:** Information on supported Goodreads pages needed to apply visual customizations and display extension features.
- **Book-cover images:** Images retrieved from supported image sources when required for book-related display features.

The extension does not intentionally collect sensitive personal information.

## 3. How Information Is Used

Information accessed by the extension is used solely to:

- Apply visual themes and customize the Goodreads interface.
- Save user preferences between browsing sessions.
- Display supported book-related information and visual features.
- Maintain the extension's functionality.

## 4. Data Storage and Sharing

Extension preferences may be stored using browser extension storage.

The extension does not sell users' personal information. It does not use personal information for advertising or behavioral profiling.

The extension developer does not receive users' browsing history through the extension as a data collection practice.

Information may be requested from Goodreads or supported image sources when necessary for features initiated through normal use of the extension. Those services may process requests under their own privacy policies.

## 5. Third-Party Services

Goodreads Glass Theme operates alongside Goodreads and may use supported external image sources for book-cover display features. These third-party services are governed by their respective privacy policies and terms.

The extension is not affiliated with or endorsed by Goodreads or Amazon.

## 6. Data Security and Retention

Preferences stored by the extension remain subject to the browser's extension storage and retention behavior. Users can remove stored preferences by clearing the extension's data or uninstalling the extension.

No online account is required to use the extension.

## 7. Children's Privacy

Goodreads Glass Theme is not designed to collect personal information from children.

This policy may be updated if the extension's functionality or data practices change. Any revised policy will be published at this URL with an updated revision date.
